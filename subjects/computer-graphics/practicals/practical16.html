Important Properties

Endpoint Interpolation

B(0) = Pâ‚€ and B(1) = Pâ‚™. The curve starts at the first control point and ends at the last.

Convex Hull Property

The Bezier curve lies within the convex hull of its control points.

Affine Invariance

Applying an affine transformation to the control points produces the same result as transforming the generated curve.

Control

Interior control points influence the direction and shape of the curve rather than normally becoming points on the curve itself.

Common Mistakes

Wrong parameter range

For the standard Bezier curve, t must remain between 0 and 1.

Wrong degree

n + 1 control points produce a degree n Bezier curve.

Missing binomial coefficient

Quadratic and cubic equations require the correct Bernstein coefficients.

Confusing control polygon and curve

Interior control points generally guide the curve; they are not necessarily points on it.

Viva Questions

Check your understanding before moving to B-Spline curves.

What is a Bezier curve?

A parametric curve defined by a set of control points and Bernstein basis functions.

What is the range of t?

For the standard Bezier curve, 0 â‰¤ t â‰¤ 1.

What determines the degree?

If there are n + 1 control points, the degree is n.

Does a Bezier curve pass through every control point?

It passes through the first and last control points. Interior control points generally influence the curve without necessarily lying on it.

What is the convex hull property?

The curve lies inside the convex hull formed by its control points.

What is a cubic Bezier curve?

A Bezier curve with four control points and degree three.

What are Bernstein polynomials?

They are the basis functions that provide the weights for the control points.

Where are Bezier curves used?

They are used in computer graphics, animation paths, fonts, UI design, CAD and shape modeling.
