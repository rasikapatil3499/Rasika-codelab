10

Important Observations

Rotation has a few properties that make it different from scaling and translation.

01

Shape

Rotation does not distort the shape of the object.

02

Size

All distances between points remain unchanged.

03

Pivot

The selected pivot remains fixed during rotation.

04

Order

When combined with other transformations, matrix order affects the result.

Viva Questions

Check your understanding before implementing the rotation algorithm.

What is 2D rotation?

It changes the orientation of a 2D object by rotating its points through a specified angle around a pivot.

What is the standard rotation matrix?

For counter-clockwise rotation, the matrix contains cosÎ¸ and sinÎ¸ terms that transform x and y coordinates.

What does a positive angle mean?

A positive angle represents counter-clockwise rotation in the standard Cartesian coordinate system.

What does a negative angle mean?

A negative angle represents clockwise rotation.

What happens to the size of an object after rotation?

The size remains unchanged because rotation preserves distances between points.

How do you rotate around a custom pivot?

Translate the pivot to the origin, rotate, and translate the object back using T(p) · R(Î¸) · T(−p).
