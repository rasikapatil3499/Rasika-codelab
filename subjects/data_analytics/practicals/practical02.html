Clean & Transform Data

Real-world datasets are rarely ready for analysis. They contain missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent text, incorrect formats and unusual values. In this practical, you will learn how to identify these problems, clean the data and transform it into an analysis-ready dataset.

01 â€” LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Make messy data usable

The goal is not simply to delete incorrect values. You must understand why the data is problematic and select an appropriate cleaning strategy.

Why data cleaning matters

Poor-quality data can produce misleading statistics, incorrect visualizations and wrong business decisions. Data cleaning is therefore one of the most important stages of the analytics workflow.

Remember: Cleaning decisions should be based on the meaning of the data, not simply on what is easiest to remove.
