Project Objective

The objective is to solve a real-world problem using data rather than simply producing charts. Students must demonstrate the complete analytical process from raw data to a final recommendation.

The final output should allow a non-technical person such as a manager, teacher, HR professional or business owner to understand the important findings quickly.

You must answer

  • What is happening in the data?
  • Which patterns are important?
  • What factors are associated with the outcome?
  • Which categories perform better or worse?
  • What action should be taken?

05 â€” ANALYSIS REQUIREMENTS

Your analysis must go beyond averages

Students should investigate multiple dimensions of the dataset instead of calculating only basic statistics.

Descriptive Analysis

  • Count and frequency
  • Mean, median and mode
  • Minimum and maximum
  • Range and standard deviation

Comparative Analysis

  • Category comparison
  • Department comparison
  • Time-period comparison
  • Top and bottom performers

Relationship Analysis

  • Correlation
  • Scatter plots
  • Variable relationships
  • Possible influencing factors

Trend Analysis

  • Monthly / yearly trends
  • Growth and decline
  • Peak periods
  • Unusual changes
