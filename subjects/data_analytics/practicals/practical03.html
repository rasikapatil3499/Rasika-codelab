PRACTICAL 03

Analyze Data & Discover Insights

Clean data is only the beginning. In this practical, you will calculate descriptive statistics, compare groups, identify trends, measure relationships and define meaningful KPIs that answer real business questions.

01 — ANALYTICS MINDSET

Don't just calculate. Ask questions.

Data analytics starts with a question. Statistics and metrics are tools used to answer that question.

01 — QUESTION

What happened?

Understand the current state of the data using totals, averages, counts and distributions.

02 — COMPARISON

Which is better?

Compare products, departments, cities, months, customer groups or other categories.

03 — RELATIONSHIP

What is connected?

Investigate whether two variables move together using correlation and other analytical techniques.

04 — DECISION

What should we do?

Convert calculations into insights that can support an actual decision.

08 — ANALYTICAL THINKING

Question → Metric

Choose a metric based on the question, rather than calculating statistics without a purpose.

What is the typical customer purchase amount?
→ Mean / Median
How much does sales vary between customers?
→ Standard Deviation
Which product generated the most revenue?
→ Sum / GroupBy
Are sales increasing over time?
→ Time-based Trend
Are study hours related to marks?
→ Correlation
Which department performs best?
→ Group Comparison
