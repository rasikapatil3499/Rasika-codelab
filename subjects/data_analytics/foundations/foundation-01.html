01 · Core Concept

What exactly is Data?

Data is not just numbers stored in an Excel sheet. It is a collection of observations, measurements, records, events, or facts that describe something about the real world.

Simple Definition

Data is raw information collected about people, objects, events, transactions, systems, or processes.


Analytics starts when we convert this raw information into meaningful insights.

Real-world examples

Student marks, hospital records, electricity consumption, online purchases, temperature readings, customer reviews, traffic counts and banking transactions are all examples of data.

05 · Real-world Thinking

Start With a Question

A good analyst does not begin by randomly creating charts. The analysis begins with a real problem.

PROBLEM

Why are students performing poorly?

A college wants to understand why some students are getting low marks. The college has attendance, internal marks, practical marks and final exam marks.

DATA NEEDED Attendance, marks, department, practical performance.
QUESTION Is attendance related to academic performance?
POSSIBLE INSIGHT Students with low attendance may require academic support.
