Build Your Analytics Foundation.

Before working with real-world datasets, understand how data is structured, prepared, explored and visualized. These five foundations provide the concepts required for every practical that follows.

02 Datasets Understand structure

05 CORE FOUNDATIONS

What You Need Before the Practicals.

These are not designed as long theoretical chapters. Each foundation introduces a concept and immediately connects it to how an analyst actually works with data.

01

What is Data Analytics?

Understand why organizations collect data and how analysts transform raw information into useful insights for decision-making.

Data vs Information

Understand the difference between raw observations and meaningful information.

What Does an Analyst Do?

Explore the role of an analyst from asking questions to finding evidence in data.

Types of Analytics

Introduction to descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics.

Business Education Healthcare Finance Retail
FOUNDATION OUTCOME

Students can explain what data analytics is and identify where analytics can be applied to real-world problems.

02

Understand Data & Datasets

Learn how datasets are organized and how different types of variables affect the analysis and visualization you can perform.

Rows & Columns

Understand observations, records, features and variables in a structured dataset.

Data Types

Work with numerical, categorical, text, date and boolean data.

Structured Data

Understand tables, CSV files, spreadsheets and database-style datasets.

Numerical Categorical Text Date Boolean
FOUNDATION OUTCOME

Students can inspect a dataset and identify its rows, columns, variables, data types and basic structure.

03

Data Quality & Cleaning

Real-world data is rarely perfect. Learn how analysts identify and handle problems before performing analysis.

Missing Values

Identify missing records and understand appropriate ways to handle them.

Duplicates

Detect repeated records that can distort counts and analytical results.

Inconsistent Data

Identify inconsistent categories, formats, spellings and invalid values.

Missing Data Duplicates Invalid Values Outliers Formatting
FOUNDATION OUTCOME

Students understand why data quality matters and can identify common problems in raw datasets before analysis.

04

Exploratory Data Analysis

Learn how analysts explore a dataset to discover distributions, patterns, relationships and unusual observations.

Descriptive Statistics

Understand count, mean, median, minimum, maximum and spread.

Distributions

Explore how values are distributed using appropriate analytical techniques.

Relationships

Investigate relationships between variables using comparison and correlation.

Mean Median Distribution Correlation Trends
FOUNDATION OUTCOME

Students can ask analytical questions and use basic exploratory techniques to identify patterns in a dataset.

05

Data Visualization & Storytelling

Learn how to transform analytical results into visual representations that make patterns and insights easier to understand.

Choosing a Chart

Understand when to use bar charts, line charts, scatter plots, histograms and other visualizations.

Visual Encoding

Learn how position, size, labels and categories communicate information visually.

Data Storytelling

Move from simply displaying charts to communicating findings and actionable insights.

Bar Chart Line Chart Scatter Plot Histogram Heatmap
FOUNDATION OUTCOME

Students can select an appropriate visualization and explain what the visual communicates about the data.
